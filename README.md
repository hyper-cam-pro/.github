## 🚀 **What is HyperCam Pro?**

HyperCam Pro is the enhanced, professional version of the classic HyperCam screen recorder. It delivers superior recording quality, advanced editing tools, and a seamless workflow — transforming your screen activity into polished, engaging video content.

---

## 🎥 **Core Features**

### **🎞️ High-Quality Recording**
- Record in up to **4K resolution** with adjustable frame rates
- Capture **full screen, specific windows, or custom regions**
- Enjoy **smooth playback** with minimal impact on system performance

### **🎙️ Professional Audio Control**
- Record **system audio and microphone** simultaneously
- Fine-tune **individual audio levels** and apply **noise reduction**
- Enhance voice clarity with **real-time audio effects**

### **✏️ Interactive On-Screen Tools**
- **Highlight mouse clicks** and **display keystrokes** during recording
- Use **drawing tools** to annotate and emphasize key points in real time
- Add **webcam overlay** for a personal touch in tutorials and presentations

### **🎬 Built-in Video Editor**
- **Trim, cut, and merge** clips without leaving the app
- Add **text, arrows, watermarks, and custom annotations**
- Create **picture-in-picture** effects for multi-source recordings

### **📤 Flexible Export Options**
- Export in **MP4, AVI, MOV, and GIF** formats
- Use **pre-set profiles** optimized for YouTube, social media, and training
- Enjoy **fast processing** with hardware-accelerated encoding
